# Bad Boss Business School — 2026 Budget Review

This repository contains the completed P&L assignment and a static website that explains the result.

## Headline result

The original draft showed €989.5k of revenue and €627.3k of operating profit, but most revenue was unsupported and most delivery costs were missing. The corrected Part 1 case produces €439.1k of revenue and a €26.5k operating loss. Three explicit management decisions recover the plan to €529.9k of revenue and €46.0k of operating profit.

## What is included

- `dist/index.html` — complete explanatory site
- `dist/styles.css` and `dist/app.js` — responsive presentation and monthly chart
- `dist/assets/Bad-Boss-Business-School-Completed-Budget.xlsx` — completed Excel workbook
- `vercel.json` — static-hosting configuration

The workbook preserves source tabs 00–08 and adds the assumption review, corrected P&L, management recommendation, management P&L, monthly comparison, calculation builds and terminal checks.

## Run locally

Serve `dist` with any static file server, for example:

```bash
npx serve dist
```

## Board recommendation

Reject the original draft. Approve the management plan as a controlled recovery, conditional on signed contractor terms, monthly lead/win tracking and external trainer bookings 6–8 weeks before delivery.
